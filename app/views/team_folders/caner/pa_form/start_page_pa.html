Apply for prior authority

Use this service to apply for prior authority for an expert or an expense. Make a new application for each one.

For experts, you only need to apply if they charge more than the codified rates and guideline hours.

As part of the application, you may need to provide:

  • the court order
  • the letter of instruction
  • an estimate of costs with a breakdown of hours
  • alternative quotes
  • the other parties' certificate references

This depends on what you apply for. We’ll make this clear as you go through the application.
