Linking cases

You can link to another application you have already submitted using this service.

A family link can be made if you are representing 2 or more family members in the proceedings. This makes sure that the cost limit is shown on one certificate only.

A legal link can be made if your client is involved in more than one court case and needs to have multiple certificates.

Linking to an application that’s already linked to another one will group them together.
